Add it in 30 seconds.

PORTAL is a remote MCP connector. You paste one URL into Claude or ChatGPT. No install, no extension, no account with us.

  1. Open Customize then Connectors, click + and Add custom connector.
  2. Name it PORTAL and paste the connector URL.
  3. Set Authentication to No sign-in and click Add.
  4. In a chat, tap +, open Connectors and turn PORTAL on.
  1. On the web, open Settings, Apps, then Advanced and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Click Create app, name it PORTAL and paste the connector URL.
  3. Choose No Authentication and create it.
  4. In a chat, tap +, pick Developer mode and select PORTAL.

Works on Claude Free, which allows one custom connector.

What it can and can't do

The connector reads the chain and builds swaps. Moving money always needs you.

  • Reads prices, liquidity and holders for any Solana token
  • Quotes and builds real swaps through Jupiter
  • Can't spend anything: every swap waits for your signature in Phantom
  • Never asks for a seed phrase or private key

Your keys never leave Phantom.

The chat can prepare a swap. Only you can send it. Every transaction is signed by you, in your own wallet, on a page that shows exactly what you're signing.

  1. 1

    You ask

    Say swap 0.5 SOL into a mint. PORTAL saves what you asked for: the token, the amount, your slippage.

  2. 2

    You get a link

    The chat replies with a signing link. It stays valid for 10 minutes.

  3. 3

    You sign in Phantom

    Connect Phantom and the page pulls a fresh Jupiter quote for your wallet: what you pay, what you get, price impact. You approve in Phantom.

  4. 4

    You check back

    Say check my swap in the chat. It reads the result on chain and links Solscan.
